What to write to us about

  • Support. Something looks wrong, a chart will not load, a connection will not link. Say which device and browser you are on and what you tapped; a screenshot helps. Never send your password, an API key or a one-time code; we will never ask for them.
  • Your data. To see, correct, export or delete what we hold, use Settings › Account in the app, or write from the e-mail address on your account. Requests are answered within 30 days, usually much faster. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Security. If you find a vulnerability, tell us at this address with enough detail to reproduce it. We will acknowledge within two business days, fix it in good faith, and credit you if you want. Please do not test against other people's accounts or data.
  • Invitations. Bull Whistle is invite-only during the beta. Existing members can ask us to invite someone by sending the address to use; the invited person then signs up with that address.
  • Data providers and partners. Licensing, attribution and partnership questions from Financial Modeling Prep, Kraken, SnapTrade and others: same address.
  • Legal notices. Formal notices under the Terms of Service may be sent to this address; we will confirm receipt by e-mail.
