The curve

Yield vs maturity at the scrubbed date — the ghost line is today, for the shape drift. Red dots mark inverted steps (that tenor yields less than the one before it).

Spreads · the recession dials

10Y−2Y filled (red below zero = inverted), 10Y−3M as the thin line. Every red patch since 1990 sat in front of a recession — 1990, 2001, 2008, 2020 — and then the 2022–24 monster with its 2025 re-steepening.

Where each one stands

Latest yields by maturity. Daily countries come from a treasury or central bank feed; the rest carry the monthly 10-year only — a dash is a number that source does not publish, never a guess. Slope = 10Y − 2Y (red = inverted). vs US = that country’s 10Y minus the US 10Y.
